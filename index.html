How Agent Strategy works

A pre-listing is a showcase of your home before it's on the market. It's how local agent experts evaluate your price and show you their strategy — and how you evaluate them.

  1. 1
    Post a pre-listing showcase

    Basic details, photos, your asking price and any preferences — an agent focused on your area, a language, a minimum experience level. Your street address stays private.

  2. 2
    Local agent experts evaluate the price

    Approved agents weigh in on whether your asking price looks too high, too low, or about right — so you hear it from people who know the market before you list.

  3. 3
    Agents share their strategy

    Each proposes a fee, what's included and how fast they can start. Compare reviews, response times and packages side by side — or invite an agent you already know.

  4. 4
    Choose, then review your agent

    Accept the strategy that fits. A shared checklist tracks the work, and afterward you can review the agent you hired.

For homeowners

  • Hear it before you list: agents weigh in on whether your asking price looks too high, too low, or about right.
  • Compare each agent's strategy — fee, included services and turnaround — side by side.
  • Ask for what matters: an agent focused on your area, a language, years of experience.
  • Already know an agent? Invite them straight to your pre-listing.

Prefer to trade than sell?

Amico Haus also lets homeowners trade homes instead of listing them. Post what you have and what would make you move, and we'll find mutual matches — including daisy chains where three or more homeowners each get what they want. Either partner in a match can ask to bring agents in to help coordinate the closing. The other partner confirms first, and each of you can choose your own agent.
