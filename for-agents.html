For local agents

Why join Amico Haus

Homeowners post their homes here before they list. Approved local agents weigh in on the price and share their strategy, and the homeowner chooses the agent whose strategy and track record fit.

Apply free See the ZIP board

Free to apply, in about 3 minutes

All it takes is your California DRE license number, your brokerage and your service area. Packages, case studies and a video introduction can be added later.

Before approving an agent, Amico Haus looks up their license on the California DRE's public license lookup. Approval needs an active California real estate license, a service area in San Diego County, and agreeing to the agent rules in the Amico Haus Terms of Use.

Be a founding agent in your ZIP

Amico Haus is launching in Chula Vista (91910, 91911, 91913, 91914 and 91915) and Bonita (91902). The first 3 approved agents in each of those ZIPs become its founding agents: a "Founding agent" ribbon on your profile and proposals, lasting while you're an approved agent serving that ZIP. Homeowners see it explained as "Among the first 3 approved agents in 91913; recognition only" (with your ZIP). In return, you aim to send a price opinion within 48 hours when a homeowner in your ZIP posts.

Founding is recognition only: no agent is promised leads, clients or business. Founding agents get alerts at the same moment as everyone else, with no head start.

Sealed price opinions

Suggest a list price for a home and back it up with recent sales and your own presentation. Price opinions are sealed: agents never see each other's numbers, so the homeowner gets independent opinions. Base your price on the home and comparable sales only.

Homeowners choose on strategy and track record

Each proposal lays out your fee, what's included and how fast you can start. Homeowners compare strategies and track records side by side, then choose. Your profile can show service packages, case studies, a video introduction and your service area.

Each agent sets their own fees; commissions are negotiable and not set by law or by Amico Haus.

Good to know
