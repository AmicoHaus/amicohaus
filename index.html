How Agent Strategy works

A pre-listing is a showcase of your home before it's on the market. It's how local agent experts evaluate your price and show you their strategy — and how you evaluate them.

  1. 1
    Post a pre-listing showcase

    Basic details, photos, your asking price and any preferences — a local specialist, a language, a minimum experience level. Your street address stays private.

  2. 2
    Local agent experts evaluate the price

    Approved agents weigh in on whether your asking price looks too high, too low, or about right — so you hear it from people who know the market before you list.

  3. 3
    Agents share their strategy

    Each proposes a fee, what's included and how fast they can start. Compare ratings, response times, win rates and packages side by side — or invite an agent you already know.

  4. 4
    Choose, then review each other

    Accept the strategy that fits. A shared checklist tracks the work, and afterward both sides review each other, so the evaluation goes both ways.

For homeowners

  • Hear it before you list: agents weigh in on whether your asking price looks too high, too low, or about right.
  • Compare each agent's strategy — fee, included services and turnaround — side by side.
  • Ask for what matters: a local specialist, a language, years of experience.
  • Already know an agent? Invite them straight to your pre-listing.

For agents

  • Apply to be vetted — an admin reviews what you report about your license and brokerage.
  • See homes before they hit the market, and say what you'd do differently.
  • Build a profile with service packages, case studies, a video introduction and your service area.
  • Win listings on your strategy and track record, and earn reviews from every client.
