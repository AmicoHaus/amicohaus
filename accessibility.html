Accessibility

Last updated November 4, 2026.

We want everyone to be able to use Amico Haus, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, screen magnification, voice control or other assistive technology.

Need help? Just ask

If anything on Amico Haus is hard to use, or you'd like information in another format, tell us and we'll help you get done what you came to do. Use the contact form, or call or text Rudy at (619) 392-6714. It helps to tell us the page you were on, what you were trying to do and what happened.

Our commitment

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as our guide, and we keep improving the site: labels on form fields, buttons and lists you can open from the keyboard, text color with enough contrast, and descriptions on photos. Some pages and tools may not work well for everyone yet, and we fix problems as we learn about them. Homes and photos are posted by members, who write their own descriptions.

Feedback

Spotted something we should fix? Please let us know through the contact form. Every note helps make Amico Haus easier to use for everyone.
