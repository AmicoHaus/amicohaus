What could your home fund instead?

Some homeowners don't want another mortgage: they'd rather sell and rent for a while instead. Rough math from your own numbers: slide each one to match your situation and see roughly how long the money from your sale could cover the rent.

Left from your sale after selling costs and your loan payoff—

An estimate only, not tax, legal or financial advice. Confirm the numbers with your agent, your lender and a tax professional.

The math is simply what's left from your sale divided by a year of rent. It doesn't count rent increases, taxes or moving costs, and it isn't a lease offer: any lease term and price are worked out directly between landlord and tenant, not set by Amico Haus. A large rent prepayment can be lost if the landlord sells, refinances or loses the home. Use escrow and a real estate attorney; California rules on advance rent may apply.
