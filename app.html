Bundle multiple properties into one portfolio trade

Own (or list on behalf of one client) more than one property? Bundle 2 or more of your active listings into a single portfolio — trade the whole group for one larger home, or for someone else's portfolio.

Hold Ctrl (or Cmd) to select more than one. Only your own active, unbundled trade listings appear here.

All bundled properties must belong to the same owner (or the same client) — a portfolio trade means exchanging everything in it at once. The combined value shown is just the sum of what you entered for each property; it isn't an appraisal. Once created, a portfolio can be dissolved (releasing every property back to its own independent listing) but not edited — dissolve and re-create it to change what's included.

Agent Strategy

A pre-listing showcase for a mutual evaluation of your home's price. Post your home before you list it and local agent experts weigh in on the asking price — too high, about right, or too low — and share their strategy for selling it: fee, included services and timeline. You compare their strategies, portfolios and reviews; they assess your home; and you review each other afterward. Already have an agent you trust? Skip all this — it's entirely optional.

Amico Haus vets agents by reviewing what they self-report (license number, brokerage) — it does not independently confirm license numbers with any state licensing board. Always verify a license yourself before engaging anyone. Nothing here is a guarantee of a sale, a fee quote, or legal/financial advice, and Amico Haus is not a party to any resulting engagement.
