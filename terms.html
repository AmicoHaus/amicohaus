As with any other listing on Amico Haus, a portfolio match does not guarantee a transaction, and any actual multi-property exchange should be documented and closed the same way any other real estate transaction would be, with appropriately licensed professionals for every property involved.

9. Home trades

Either partner in a mutual match can ask to open their trade to agent proposals. The other partner confirms first: until then, no agent is told about it or can see it, and no one can be hired on it. One agent for both homes, or two agents under the same brokerage, is dual agency and needs both owners' written consent. Each of you can choose your own agent. After both partners confirm, either partner can accept a proposal, so talk with your trade partner before accepting. Accepting marks the proposal as chosen on Amico Haus; any agency relationship still takes a written agreement with the agent's brokerage.
