Agent Strategy

A pre-listing is a showcase of a home before it's listed. Local agent experts evaluate the asking price — too high, about right, or too low — and present their strategy for selling it: fee, included services and timeline. It's a mutual evaluation: the homeowner weighs each agent's strategy and track record, the agents assess the home and its price, and both sides review each other when the work is done. A homeowner can also open a matched trade to agents, or simply keep working with an agent they already know.

Everything in this section is an illustrative walkthrough with fictional agents. It is not pulled from real accounts. In the live marketplace, agents are reviewed by an admin based on what they report about themselves, license numbers are not independently verified with any state board, and no payment runs through Amico Haus.
