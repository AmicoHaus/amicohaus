Plan your move

Sell first or buy first?

Moving from one home to the next comes down to timing. Here are the three common ways to line it up, and a quick way to see your own numbers.

Sell first

You know exactly what you have to spend, and you never carry two mortgages. The trick is having somewhere to land.

  • Ask for a rent-back. Many buyers will let you stay a few weeks after closing while you find your next place.
  • Rent short-term while you shop, then buy with your sale money in hand, which makes for a strong offer.

Buy first

You move once, on your schedule, and can take your time getting your current home ready to sell.

  • Make an offer contingent on selling your home. It protects you, though some sellers prefer offers without one.
  • Use your home's equity for the down payment. A home equity line of credit or a bridge loan can do that; a lender can tell you what you'd qualify for.

Close both the same week

Your agent lines up both escrows so your sale funds your purchase. It takes coordination, and it's the cleanest move when it works.
