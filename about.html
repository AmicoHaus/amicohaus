About Amico Haus

Amico Haus is presented by a local agent, built so homeowners can show their home to local agent experts before listing, hear what they think of the price, and compare the strategy each would use.

Rudy Flores

Real Estate Strategist, Coldwell Banker West

DRE #02257808

Rudy works with buyers and sellers throughout San Diego County, including EastLake and Chula Vista, and built Amico Haus around one idea: a pre-listing showcase where local agent experts evaluate a home's price and lay out their strategy — fee, included services and how fast they can start — before the homeowner commits, and where good agents can win work on their track record. The evaluation is mutual: agents assess the home, the homeowner assesses each agent, and both review each other afterward. Homeowners who'd rather trade than list can do that here too, with a licensed agent involved every step of the way.

How Amico Haus fits in

Every request on this platform is created by a real homeowner, and every agent's view and strategy comes from an agent who has applied and been reviewed by an admin. Price feedback is an opinion, not an appraisal. Amico Haus doesn't replace the relationship between a homeowner and their agent — it's a way to find the right one faster, compare them fairly, and keep the work on track. No payments run through Amico Haus: the two sides handle fees directly, the way they would anyway. Trades and matches are worked through with a real estate professional too — your own agent or Rudy directly — before anything is finalized.
