Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: name, email, and password (stored as a salted, hashed value — we never store or can see your actual password), plus a phone number if you choose to add one, and which version of the Terms of Use and this policy you agreed to, and when.
  • Your contact choices: how agents can reach you by message, your quiet hours, whether your phone number shows publicly, and a record of each agent you chose to share your contact info with and when you shared it.
  • Notifications on your device: if you turn on notifications, we store the address your browser gives us for sending them. They carry no details, and they're delivered through your browser maker's push service (such as Google, Apple or Mozilla).
  • Listing information: your home's address, city, property details, and estimated value, plus what you're looking for in a trade, and any optional "why you're moving" tags you pick (such as "Need more space" or "Relocating").
  • Pre-listing information: your home's details, photos, walkthrough video, asking price and costs, plus any preferences for your agent, such as a language you'd like them to speak or a minimum number of years of experience. If you interview agents, your interview times, scorecard and notes. Also your ideal closing window and how long you'd like to stay after closing, your weekly check-in answers (how ready you feel and what's on your mind), and when you ask your agents for fresh price opinions. Also, if you use them: your Then & Now upgrade list (with any permit answers) and photo pairs, your handoff binder notes, and which photos you marked "Sweep done."
  • Home facts you add to a listing (yard and sun, parking and garage, pets, floor plan). They show on the listing as your own words; your floor plan is listed with other homes only if you turn that on.
  • Saved searches and alerts: the places, home types and price ranges you save, and any move-in window and stay-after days you add, so we can tell you about new homes, and any AugmentedHomes alert listing the home adaptations you need.
  • Agent applications: your license number, brokerage, service area and profile details, and a photo of your license if you choose to upload one.
  • Reviews: if you review an agent you worked with outside Amico Haus, your rating and review, whether you bought or sold, the year, the city and, if you add it, the street address.
  • Activity: posts, comments, likes, messages, blocks, and reports you submit.
  • Contact messages: the name, email, optional phone number and message you send through the contact form.
  • How you found us: if you arrive from an ad, a video or another site, the campaign tags in that link (such as utm_source), any ad-click ID the platform added to it (Facebook/Instagram, Google or TikTok), the page you landed on, the name of the referring site, and whether you arrived inside an app's built-in browser (TikTok, Instagram or Facebook). This is kept in your browser for up to 30 days and saved with your account or message only if you sign up or write to us. If you join from a member's invite link, we record who invited you; they see only a count.
  • Your display language (English or Spanish), if you pick one. Only you see it; we use it only to show the Spanish pages and sign-up emails in that language, never to choose which homes or agents you see.
  • Technical data: your IP address, used to limit abuse of logging in, signing up and the contact form (not to track you). Records of login and signup attempts are deleted after about two days; a message sent through the contact form is kept with the IP address it came from. We also see anonymous page-view counts from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or identify you.

2. How we use it

To run the matching engine, show your listing to other users at the privacy level you choose, operate the social feed and messaging features, send the alerts you ask for, and protect the platform from abuse (spam, brute-force login attempts). "How you found us" information is used only to see which posts, videos and ads bring people to Amico Haus; it isn't shared with ad platforms or used to show you ads. If you have a pre-listing, we send one short email on Mondays about activity on your home on Amico Haus; stop it with one click from the email or in your account settings.

3. Who can see what

Public pages are on the open web. Active listings, AugmentedHomes and GreenHomes pages, and approved agents' profiles can be seen by anyone, and search engines and other web crawlers (including AI tools) can index them. Homeowner profile pages can be opened by anyone with the link but are marked so search engines leave them out, and FinderMine project pages need an account. Posts in the community feed can also be read without an account. Your display name is visible to other users on your posts, comments, listings and matches.

Your exact street address is never shown to other users — only your city and, if you choose to share it, your neighborhood — unless you share your contact info with an agent on a pre-listing, open a broker preview, or turn on address sharing for one of your open houses (all below). "Why you're moving" tags are left off public listing pages and flyers.

Pre-listings are shared only with approved agents. Your pre-listing's details, photos and walkthrough video can be opened only by you, approved local agents and Amico Haus admins. Other members see only a short preview: the city and ZIP, home type, beds and baths, and your asking price. Once a pre-listing is no longer open, only agents who already took part in it can still open it. If you open a broker preview on a pre-listing, approved agents who RSVP to it can see your street address until the preview ends, and you're told each time an agent RSVPs. An agent's price opinion, comps, notes and presentation file are shown only to the homeowner, never to other agents, and a homeowner's interview scorecard and notes are seen only by that homeowner.

Timing, check-ins and price opinion history. Approved agents who can open your pre-listing see your closing window and stay-after days, how many photos you added, whether there's a walkthrough video or an upcoming broker preview, and your Decision Night date. Other members never see your dates; a member whose saved search's move-in window and stay fit yours sees a "Timing fits" tag on your short preview. Your listing-ready checklist count, target list month and how ready you feel (Exploring, Getting close or Ready) are shown to agents only if you turn on "Share my progress with agents." The number you pick and what's on your mind in a weekly check-in are never shown to agents or other members. We use them to order the tools on your pre-listing and, only as totals across at least 20 homeowners (never per person, per home or per area), to see what homeowners want help with. Every version of an agent's price opinion on your home is kept while the pre-listing exists, and only you and that agent see it; agents never see each other's numbers.

Privacy Sweep, Then & Now, visit feedback and your handoff binder. Privacy Sweep blurs or covers parts of a photo on your device before it uploads, and the swept copy is the only one we receive. Where your browser offers face suggestions, they are worked out on your device and never sent to us. The "Sweep done" mark is shown only to you. Approved agents who can open your pre-listing see your Then & Now list and photo pairs; your permit answers are shown only to you, the agent you hire and agents you shared your contact info with. Agents who visit through an accepted showing or a broker preview can leave three quick answers about the home; only you see them, with the agent's name and DRE number, and they are never public or added up across homes. Your handoff binder is saved privately for you to print or save as a PDF; we never send it to anyone or show it to agents. If you tick "Rudy Flores may repost" on a Decision Night moment, we keep the date and the wording you agreed to and Rudy gets a short note; you can take it back anytime.

Rudy and Amico Haus admins. When you post a pre-listing, Rudy Flores, who runs Amico Haus and also works as a real estate agent, gets an email with your name, email and the home's basics so he can run the site. He doesn't use it to contact you about your home unless you share your contact info with him, the same as any agent. Amico Haus admins can see account details to run the site.

Agent applications. If you apply to be an approved agent and choose to upload a license photo, only you and Amico Haus admins can view it — it is never shown to homeowners or other agents.

Reviews of agents. A review you leave for an agent you worked with outside Amico Haus shows your name, the year, the city and whether you bought or sold. The street address, if you add it, is kept private: Amico Haus uses it only if a review is reported as fake, and never to contact you about real estate.

Saved searches and alerts are private to you. Saved searches are also counted, never named, in the "buyers looking" numbers homeowners and agents see, and small numbers show as "a few" or not at all. AugmentedHomes alerts about the home adaptations you need are used only to alert you and are never shown to anyone else. A move-in window you add is used only to show you "Timing fits" tags and to count, never name, buyers whose timing fits a homeowner's (shown only once there are 3 or more).

Your phone number is private by default. It appears on your public profile and listings only if you turn on "Show my phone number" in You're in Control; while it's off, people reach you through Amico Haus messages. Your email address is shown only to agents you share your contact info with.

Sharing with an agent you like. When you tap "Share my contact info" on an agent's proposal or price opinion, that agent can see your exact address, phone number and email for that home, so they can reach you directly. We record when you shared. You can stop sharing anytime and they won't see it on Amico Haus anymore (anything they already noted down stays with them).

Who saw my address. Every agent who has seen your exact address, through your share or by RSVPing to a broker preview you opened, is listed for you with the first and last time they saw it. That list is kept while the pre-listing exists and is deleted with it.

Open houses: if you turn on "Share the street address with people who RSVP" for an open house, people who RSVP'd see that address from 24 hours before it starts until it ends, and you see the display names of the people going. When you RSVP to someone else's open house, its owner sees your display name (RSVPs made before November 11, 2026 show only in the count). Blocking either way hides the address and your name from each other.

Blocking. You can block anyone. A block works both ways: you can't message each other, and a blocked agent can't open your pre-listings or send you proposals, price opinions, or showing or interview requests. Blocking also ends any contact info shared between you, their RSVPs to your upcoming broker previews, pending showing requests, upcoming interviews and pending invites between you, and closes any open trade request between the two of you. Proposals and price opinions they already sent are kept but left out of your lists, and come back if you unblock.

Photos and videos. Phones save the place a photo or video was taken inside the file. Amico Haus removes that location (along with other hidden details, such as the phone model) from every photo and video you upload, before it's stored, so a picture of your kitchen never gives away your address. A few drones and action cameras also record a position track inside the video itself; for those, upload an edited copy.

4. You're in Control: how agents reach you

You decide how agents can reach you by message: Open (anytime), Weekly (one message per agent, delivered together on Sunday morning) or Quiet (no agent messages; you still get price opinions and proposals). Agents you already work with, such as one you messaged, shared your contact info with, hired, invited or booked an interview with, always reach you as usual. With quiet hours set, emails wait until your quiet hours end and phone and browser notifications aren't sent; in-app alerts still arrive right away. While a pre-listing is open, you can also pause new proposals on it (agents who already sent one can still update theirs). You can change any of these whenever you like, and the same rules apply to every agent, including the agent who runs Amico Haus.

5. Data sharing

We don't sell your data. We share it only with other members as described in §3 (for example, your pre-listing with approved agents, who are independent agents at their own brokerages, and your contact info with an agent you choose to share it with), and with the service providers that run the site: Cloudflare (hosting, storage and anonymous page-view counts) and our email delivery service (account emails and the alerts you ask for), and your browser maker's push service if you turn on notifications. Some pages also load map images or code straight from another service (OpenStreetMap's map tiles, Cloudflare's code library), which sees your IP address the way any website you visit does.

When you tap Plan your drive, Directions or Open the whole route, the area or address you picked opens in Google Maps or Apple Maps under their privacy policies. We don't send it to them ourselves.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your listing at any time from the app. A deleted listing stays in Recently Deleted for 7 days so you can restore it, then it's removed for good. Deleting a pre-listing also deletes its photos, its contact-share records and its "Who saw my address" list. To delete your account entirely, use Delete My Account on the Account Security page (or ask through the contact form) — this removes your account, listings, pre-listings, photos, posts, and messages. You can also download a copy of your data with Export My Data on the Account Security page.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 and a unique salt per account; we never store plaintext passwords. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. Login and signup attempts are rate-limited to reduce brute-force and spam abuse.

8. Cookies, browser storage and Do Not Track

We use a single essential cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use advertising cookies or ad-platform tracking pixels. Your browser's local storage keeps the "how you found us" details described above for up to 30 days, plus conveniences such as recently viewed homes, your last open tab and unsent form drafts; you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Your Mystery Home Monday guesses are kept only in your browser's local storage; we never receive them.

Places you type into "Plan your drive" and the order of your open-house plan stay in your browser's storage; we don't receive them.

Do Not Track. Amico Haus doesn't use cross-site tracking or advertising tools, and we haven't added any that would let other companies follow your activity across websites. The site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and show account holders a notice in the app the next time they open it.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access, correct or delete your data, can be sent through the contact form.
