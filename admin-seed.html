Admin Tools

Checking admin access…

Moderation Queue

User-submitted reports on posts, comments, listings and people (including "Report a rules problem" and fair housing concerns). Taking something down hides it instead of deleting it, needs a reason, and tells the author why.

Pending Agent Applications

Application review for the Pre-Listings Marketplace — before approving, open the agent's DRE record and check the license is active, the name matches and the responsible broker matches their brokerage. Approving marks the license as checked on the DRE today.

License re-checks

Approved agents, oldest DRE check first. Re-check each license every month or so: open the record, confirm it's still active with the same broker, then mark it checked.

Past-client reviews

Newest first. Remove one only if it breaks the rules (fake, abusive, or about who lives somewhere rather than the agent's work). Removals are on the activity log; agents can't remove reviews themselves, and an admin can't decide on reviews of their own agent profile. The reviewer's email and street address show only after someone who isn't an admin has reported that review.

Reviews of agents hired here

Newest first, then removed ones. Remove or restore a review only with a reason: it goes on the activity log and the reviewer is told. Amico Haus is run by Rudy Flores, a Coldwell Banker West agent who may compete for the same homes, so on a home where an admin account sent a proposal the review shows "Recused" instead of the buttons.

Suspend or Reinstate an Account

A suspended account is signed out everywhere, can't log in and drops out of every list. A suspended agent gives up their founding spots; when reinstated they get one back only if one is open. Admin accounts can't be suspended here.

Reactivate a Paused Listing

A listing taken down from the Moderation Queue stays paused, and its owner can't reactivate it. Once the problem is fixed, reactivate it here by its listing ID (it goes on the activity log).

Dispute History (read-only)

Disputes filed before they were replaced by "Report a rules problem." Read-only history: Amico Haus doesn't decide disputes between homeowners and agents, so there are no verdicts or new notes. Fee or service problems go to the agent's managing broker, the California DRE or small claims court.

Admin Activity Log

A record of moderation and account actions taken by admins.

Generate Demo Data

Seeded accounts use @demo.amicohaus.local emails and no password — they're for demo purposes only and can be removed later without touching real accounts. Listings are concentrated in California, Florida, New York, and Washington so a smaller batch still produces plenty of overlap.

Growth, Last 30 Days

Real signups and new listings (combined across all 4 marketplaces), by day. Demo accounts excluded.

Ready Meter totals

Homeowners' latest weekly check-in from the last 90 days. Totals show once 20 homeowners have checked in, and are never shown per home or per area.

Mystery Home Monday

A weekly guessing game about a home that already sold, shown at /mystery-home. A home stays a draft until you schedule it, and it can only be scheduled with the seller's written OK date, Coldwell Banker West's OK date, 3 or more photos checked in Privacy Sweep, the photo rights, and a reveal at least 24 hours after it publishes. Once it's public it can only be retired; reveals retire on their own after 8 weeks.

Add a Mystery Home

City only. Never MLS photos or remarks, never who listed or sold it. Describe the home, not who should live there or who lives nearby.

The home only: no street, ZIP, subdivision or community name, schools or who lives nearby.

Homeowners who OK'd Rudy reposting a Decision Night moment (last 180 days). They send the recording themselves. When one is taken back, remove those reposts.
