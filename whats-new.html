What's new

Everything that's missing from real estate — as we build it.

A running list of what's shipped recently. No fluff, no "stay tuned" — just what's actually live right now.

Market Pulse

A live activity ticker across every marketplace — new listings, price drops, accepted offers, open houses — the moment they actually happen. Nothing here is ever simulated; real estate doesn't tick second-by-second like a stock, so this only ever logs real events. Includes a trending panel, a weekly leaderboard (biggest price drop, hottest listing), and a city/state filter so you can scope it to one neighborhood.

Open Market Pulse →

Discussion threads

Every listing — including public ones, no sign-in required to read — now has its own discussion thread. Ask a question, get an answer, upvote the useful ones. It's the same comment system across AugmentedHomes, GreenHomes, FinderMine, and regular listings.

Price-history sparklines

A quick visual of a home's real price history, right on its card — not just the detail page. Paired with a trend badge, days-on-market, price-per-sqft, and a "price cut" or "just listed" accent when something real happened in the last 48 hours.

Save a watch

Set up an alert for exactly what you care about — a price drop, a new listing, a specific city — and get notified in-app the moment a real event matches it. No digging through the feed.

Compare mode

Pick up to three AugmentedHomes or GreenHomes listings from a browse grid and see them side by side — price, sqft, price history, features — without opening tabs back and forth. Your comparison is saved on this device, and you can copy a share link to send it to someone else.
