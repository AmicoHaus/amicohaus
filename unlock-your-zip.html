Be a founding agent

The first 3 approved agents in each ZIP become its founding agents: a "Founding agent" ribbon on your profile and proposals, lasting while you're an approved agent serving that ZIP. In return, you aim to send a price opinion within 48 hours when a homeowner in your ZIP posts. It's recognition only: founding agents get alerts at the same moment as everyone else and no head start.

How we approve agents: an active California real estate license (we look it up on the DRE's public lookup), a service area in San Diego County, and following the Amico Haus agent rules. Applying takes about 3 minutes and it's free.
