What's new

Everything that's missing from the real estate market for the homeowner — as we build it.

A running list of what's shipped recently. No fluff, no "stay tuned" — just what's actually live right now.

Bring your reviews, and share your stats

Agents can now send past clients a review link from their own email or phone, so a new profile doesn't start at zero. Those reviews show in their own "Past clients, outside Amico Haus" section, apart from reviews from homeowners who hire them here. Agents can also download a Brag Card and add a live badge to their website, showing what Amico Haus records: hires, finalist spots, reviews from homeowners who hired them, and proposal speed.

Join as an agent →

Decision Night and pitch reels

Homeowners can now pick a Decision Night: every proposal opens at once, so the first one in doesn't set the bar for the rest. Agents can add a 15 to 60 second pitch reel made just for that home, and homeowners swipe through them to shortlist or pass. After hiring, a homeowner can tell their finalists what tipped the decision (never a fee), every agent who wasn't picked hears that it was decided, and agent profiles show how many homes they were a finalist on.

Post a pre-listing →

Agents: apply in 3 minutes, and be a founding agent

Applying now takes your California DRE license number, brokerage and service area, and that's it; the rest can be added later, and a progress bar shows what homeowners compare. We look up every license on the DRE's public lookup before approving. The first 3 approved agents in each launch ZIP become its founding agents, and a live board shows open pre-listings, buyers watching each city and the agents serving each ZIP.

See the ZIP board →

Get ready to sell, step by step

Your pre-listing now comes with seller prep tools. Agents can tell you what they'd fix before listing, and you see it ranked by how many agree. Add a short phone video walkthrough so agents can weigh in without a visit, open a broker preview window agents can RSVP to, and interview your top agents with a private scorecard. A "get listing-ready" checklist counts down to the date you'd like to list.

Selling and buying? Plan which to do first →

Sealed price opinions and your take-home

Agents weighing in on your pre-listing can now suggest a list price, back it up with recent sales and attach their own presentation. It's sealed: agents never see each other's numbers, so you get independent opinions, plus the range and middle once three are in. A take-home estimate turns each agent's fee into what you'd actually keep, and you'll see how many buyers on Amico Haus are looking for a home like yours.

Mello-Roos and HOA, up front

Homes now show Mello-Roos and HOA dues, what the dues include, and the HOA's rental and pet rules, and you can filter for homes with no Mello-Roos or an HOA under your budget.

Vet agents your way

The agent directory now separates what we measure on Amico Haus — times hired here, how fast an agent sends a proposal, reviews — from what agents say about themselves. Filter by pricing (flat fee or commission), past work, languages and more, and sort by rating, most hired or fastest proposal. When proposals come in on your pre-listing, you can sort and narrow them the same way.

Market Pulse

A live activity ticker across every marketplace — new listings, price drops, accepted offers, open houses — the moment they actually happen. Nothing here is ever simulated; real estate doesn't tick second-by-second like a stock, so this only ever logs real events. Includes a trending panel, a weekly leaderboard (biggest price drop, hottest listing), and a city/state filter so you can scope it to one neighborhood.

Open Market Pulse →

Discussion threads

Every listing — including public ones, no sign-in required to read — now has its own discussion thread. Ask a question, get an answer, upvote the useful ones. It's the same comment system across AugmentedHomes, GreenHomes, FinderMine, and regular listings.

Price-history sparklines

A quick visual of a home's real price history, right on its card — not just the detail page. Paired with a trend badge, days-on-market, price-per-sqft, and a "price cut" or "just listed" accent when something real happened in the last 48 hours.

Save a watch

Set up an alert for exactly what you care about — a price drop, a new listing, a specific city — and get notified in-app the moment a real event matches it. No digging through the feed.

Compare mode

Pick up to three AugmentedHomes or GreenHomes listings from a browse grid and see them side by side — price, sqft, price history, features — without opening tabs back and forth. Your comparison is saved on this device, and you can copy a share link to send it to someone else.
