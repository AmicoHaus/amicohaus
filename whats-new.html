Privacy Sweep, Then & Now, and tools for after you hire

Blur or cover faces, family photos, mail and house numbers on your phone before your photos upload. Show agents what you've done to your home with Then & Now: an old photo next to today's, and a dated list of your upgrades (owner-reported, not a disclosure).

Agents who visit can leave three quick taps about the home, just for you. A short Monday email sums up the week on your home. Once you hire, make a Hired! story for your socials, and fill in a handoff binder to print for the next owner.

New every Monday: Mystery Home Monday, a guessing game about a home that already sold. Just for fun.

Timing, a weekly check-in and fresh price opinions

Pick your ideal closing window and how long you'd like to stay after closing, up to 29 days. Your agents write the rent-back terms. Buyers can add a move-in window to a saved search, and once 3 or more interested buyers' timing fits yours, you'll see how many.

A two-tap weekly check-in moves the tools for what's on your mind to the top of your dashboard. "What agents see" shows the facts agents see on your pre-listing, and you choose whether to share your progress.

Every 90 days, ask the agents who gave you a price opinion for a fresh look. They answer Same or Update in one tap, and you keep a private history of each agent's opinion. Every number is an agent price opinion, not an appraisal.

More control, sharper alerts

Block any agent in one tap, right from their proposal, price opinion, showing request, interview or message thread. They won't be able to reach you or see your homes, and you can unblock anytime in Account › Blocked Users.

Saved searches now take a min price as well as a max, and an alert fires when a new listing shows up in the neighborhood you named, with a link straight to it. You can keep up to 10.

Pre-listings are now shared only with approved local agents. Everyone else sees a short preview, and anyone thinking of selling can post their own for free.

Post without worry: you're in control

Post your home on your own terms. Pick how agents can reach you: Open (anytime), Weekly (one message per agent, delivered together Sunday morning) or Quiet (price opinions and proposals only). Set quiet hours, and emails and phone alerts wait until they end, while in-app alerts still arrive right away. Pause new proposals whenever you like; agents who already sent one can still update it.

Your phone number and exact address stay private until you tap "Share my contact info" on an agent you like, and you can stop sharing anytime. "Who saw my address" lists every agent who has seen it, through your share or a broker preview you opened. Your phone number is now private by default too, and every agent who reaches you shows their name, DRE license number and brokerage.

Not sure what to ask? Leave the asking price blank, and local agents will tell you what they think your home is worth.

On-duty hours and your week, every Monday

Agents can set their on-duty hours: new-request emails that arrive after hours wait until they're back, while in-app alerts still reach every agent at the same moment. Homeowners can see how many agents who serve their ZIP are on duty right now. And every Monday morning, agents get their week: proposals, price opinions, interviews, new homes nearby, how their proposal speed compares, and one next step.

Bring your reviews, and share your stats

Agents can now send past clients a review link from their own email or phone, so a new profile doesn't start at zero. Those reviews show in their own "Past clients, outside Amico Haus" section, apart from reviews from homeowners who hire them here. Agents can also download a Brag Card and add a live badge to their website, showing what Amico Haus records: hires, finalist spots, reviews from homeowners who hired them, and proposal speed.

Decision Night and pitch reels

Homeowners can now pick a Decision Night: every proposal opens at once, so the first one in doesn't set the bar for the rest. Agents can add a 15 to 60 second pitch reel made just for that home, and homeowners swipe through them to shortlist or pass. After hiring, a homeowner can tell their finalists what tipped the decision (never a fee), every agent who wasn't picked hears that it was decided, and agent profiles show how many homes they were a finalist on.

Agents: apply in 3 minutes, and be a founding agent

Applying now takes your California DRE license number, brokerage and service area, and that's it; the rest can be added later, and a progress bar shows what homeowners compare. We look up every license on the DRE's public lookup before approving. The first 3 approved agents in each launch ZIP become its founding agents (recognition only), and a live board shows open pre-listings, buyers watching each city and the agents serving each ZIP.

Get ready to sell, step by step

Your pre-listing now comes with seller prep tools. Agents can tell you what they'd fix before listing, and you see it ranked by how many agree. Add a short phone video walkthrough so agents can weigh in without a visit, open a broker preview window agents can RSVP to, and interview your top agents with a private scorecard. A "get listing-ready" checklist counts down to the date you'd like to list.

Sealed price opinions and your take-home

Agents weighing in on your pre-listing can now suggest a list price, back it up with recent sales and attach their own presentation. It's sealed: agents never see each other's numbers, so you get independent opinions, plus the low-to-high range once three are in. A take-home estimate turns each agent's fee into dollars using your own numbers, and you'll see how many buyers on Amico Haus are looking for a home like yours.

The agent directory now separates what we measure on Amico Haus — times hired here, how fast an agent sends a proposal, reviews — from what agents say about themselves. Market Pulse

A live activity ticker across every marketplace — new listings, price drops, accepted offers (not closed sales), open houses — the moment they actually happen. Nothing here is ever simulated; real estate doesn't tick second-by-second like a stock, so this only ever logs real events. Includes a trending panel, a weekly "most activity" card, and a city/state filter so you can scope it to one neighborhood.

Discussion threads

Every listing now has its own discussion thread for signed-in members. Ask a question, get an answer, upvote the useful ones. It's the same comment system across AugmentedHomes, GreenHomes, FinderMine, and regular listings.

Price-history sparklines

A quick visual of the owner's price changes, right on its card — not just the detail page. Paired with a trend badge, days-on-market, price-per-sqft, and a "price cut" or "just listed" accent when something real happened in the last 48 hours.

Save a watch

Set up an alert for exactly what you care about — a price drop, a new listing, a specific city — and get notified in-app the moment a real event matches it. No digging through the feed.

Compare mode

Pick up to three AugmentedHomes or GreenHomes listings from a browse grid and see them side by side — price, sqft, price history, features — without opening tabs back and forth. Your comparison is saved on this device, and you can copy a share link to send it to someone else.
