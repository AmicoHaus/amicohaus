6. Rental listings and lease-term estimates

Amico Haus also lets users list a home for rent instead of trade, and shows sellers a suggested lease-term range for a landlord's rental listing based on the seller's stated home value. That range is a rough, illustrative estimate only — it is not an appraisal, valuation, financial or tax advice, a lease offer, or a promise of any specific term. The low end is a simple calculation (estimated net sale proceeds divided by annual rent); the high end adds an additional amount of time on the theory that a landlord receiving payment as a lump sum upfront avoids the vacancy and collection risk of collecting rent monthly, and is meant only as a starting point for negotiation between the parties. Amico Haus is not a party to, and does not set, arrange, guarantee, or broker, any lease, rental term, or lump-sum payment — any actual lease is a private agreement between landlord and tenant, and should be put in writing and reviewed the same as any other lease.

7. Portfolio listings

Amico Haus lets a user bundle two or more of their own active listings into a single "portfolio" — a single tradeable unit intended to be exchanged as a whole, for one larger home or for another user's portfolio. Creating a portfolio does not create any new legal entity, joint ownership, or escrow arrangement; it is a display and matching convenience only, and the combined value shown for a portfolio is simply the sum of the values the user entered for each property, not an appraisal. Every property that goes into a portfolio must belong to the same underlying owner (the same client, or the user's own home) — bundling properties on behalf of different owners without their agreement to trade everything together is not permitted. As with any other listing on Amico Haus, a portfolio match does not guarantee a transaction, and any actual multi-property exchange should be documented and closed the same way any other real estate transaction would be, with appropriately licensed professionals for every property involved.
